# CF Liepāja proposal

## Scope
Private Latvian proposal at /cf-liepaja. It uses the existing Nordora header, footer, tokens, and buttons. Persuade mode: the club should see a sanza station in their gym and email Sascha.

## Audience and job
Gym owners opening a link, often on a phone. The first screen is the installation image. Then the equipment, the member path, how it fits the club, an illustrative gross figure, and a demonstration request.

## Constraints
No medical or performance claims. No club-owned photographs. No CF Liepāja logo. Five concept images only, each labeled “Koncepta vizualizācija”, with one clarification. Page stays noindex. Do not invent prices, payback, or a free trial.

## Direction
The Quiet Treatment Room, same as the rest of Nordora Vital. Warm beige, deep green close, moss and mauve buttons. sanza stays lowercase. Nordora is the presenting brand.
